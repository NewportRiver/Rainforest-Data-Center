# Contributing

Contributions from data-center engineers, ecologists, hydrologists, soil scientists, energy modelers, Indigenous and local communities, and life-cycle assessment practitioners are welcome.

## Ground rules

- Distinguish measured results from estimates and hypotheses.
- State system boundaries, baselines, sources, and uncertainty.
- Do not propose clearing intact habitat or diverting scarce community water.
- Flag safety, biosecurity, land-rights, and environmental-justice concerns early.
- Prefer reproducible models and open formats.

Open an issue before a large change. For model changes, include a small example and expected output.
