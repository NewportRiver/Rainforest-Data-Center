# Development roadmap

## Phase 0 — Define (0–3 months)

- Establish system boundaries and counterfactual baselines.
- Select two contrasting climate archetypes.
- Build hourly energy, heat, rainfall, and water-demand datasets.
- Identify environmental and community veto criteria.

## Phase 1 — Model (3–9 months)

- Develop coupled energy-water-carbon and microclimate models.
- Compare air, evaporative, hybrid, and liquid cooling.
- Run drought, heat-wave, grid-outage, and flood scenarios.
- Publish assumptions, uncertainty, and negative results.

## Phase 2 — Bench pilot (9–18 months)

- Instrument a small liquid-cooled compute rack.
- Test heat recovery for drying or a controlled greenhouse loop.
- Test rainwater, condensate, and wetland-polishing loops separately.
- Perform biological safety and water-quality review.

## Phase 3 — Field demonstrator (18–36 months)

- Deploy on degraded land at community-scale power.
- Restore native vegetation with control plots.
- Operate through at least one wet and one dry season.
- Use third-party verification for environmental claims.

## Phase 4 — Replicate or stop

- Replicate only subsystems with demonstrated net benefit.
- Publish failure thresholds and decommissioning obligations.
- Adapt designs to local ecology; do not export a universal template.
